Why OriginTag

AI authenticity check

Vision AI scores every item 0–100. Scores below 70 — like a single catalog photo — go to manual review instead of getting a passport.

BEP-721 passport

Brand, serial, warranty and score are minted as an NFT to the owner’s wallet. Photo evidence is stored on BNB Greenfield.

Escrow marketplace

Buyer pays, NFT moves, seller gets paid — atomically, in one transaction. A 2% platform fee, overpayments refunded.

Permanent history

Every transfer and sale is recorded by the contract itself, so the chain of ownership can never be broken or edited.

Recalls & service records

Brands can flag recalled batches instantly, and repairs stay attached to the passport for the next owner to see.

No seed phrase

Sign in with Google or email through Web3Auth. A wallet is created for you, and transactions are signed on your device.

How it works

  1. 01

    Snap & register

  2. 02

    AI verifies

    Vision AI scores authenticity. 70+ passes, the rest go to review.

  3. 03

    Passport minted

    A BEP-721 passport lands in your wallet on BNB Chain.

  4. 04

    Resell with escrow

    List it in the marketplace. Ownership history updates itself.
